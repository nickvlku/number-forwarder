**Program name:** THE VLKU call and voicemail notifications.

**Description:** The Service sends text messages to a single recipient, the owner's own mobile phone, to notify them of new voicemails (with a transcript excerpt and a link to the dashboard) and to relay copies of text messages received at 415-THE-VLKU. No marketing or promotional messages are sent.

**Opt-in:** The owner opts in by configuring their mobile number in the Service. No other person is enrolled.

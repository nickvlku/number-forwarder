# Privacy Policy

**Effective:** September 5, 2026

This policy covers the call-forwarding and messaging service operated by Nick Vlku for the phone number 415-THE-VLKU (+1 415 843 8558), referred to below as "the Service".

## What the Service does

The Service forwards calls made to 415-THE-VLKU to the owner's personal mobile phone, records voicemail when a call is not answered, transcribes those voicemails, and stores text messages sent to the number so the owner can read them in a private dashboard. The Service also sends text-message notifications to the owner's own mobile phone.

## Information collected

When you call or text 415-THE-VLKU, the Service records:

- your phone number and the time of the call or message;
- for calls, whether the call was answered, how long it lasted, and any voicemail you leave, including an automated transcript of that voicemail;
- for text messages, the content of the message and any attached media.

The owner may add a name and notes to a phone number inside the private dashboard.

## How information is used

Information is used only to operate the Service for its owner: to display call and message history, to play back voicemail, and to notify the owner that a call or message arrived. It is not used for advertising or sold to anyone.

## Text messaging

The Service sends text messages to one recipient only: the mobile phone the owner has configured. Those messages are notifications about voicemails and relayed copies of texts sent to 415-THE-VLKU. The Service does not send marketing messages to anyone.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

## Service providers

The Service runs on infrastructure provided by Twilio (telephony and messaging), Fly.io (hosting), and OpenAI (voicemail transcription). Each provider processes data only as needed to deliver its part of the Service, under its own privacy terms. Audio recordings are stored by Twilio and fetched by the Service for playback and transcription.

## Retention

Call records, voicemails, transcripts, and messages are kept until the owner deletes them or discontinues the Service.

## Your choices

If you do not want your call recorded, hang up before the tone. To have your information removed, contact the owner at the address below.
